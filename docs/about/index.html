About.

Hardware engineer thinking at the intersection of computing, verification, and intelligence.

Hi, I’m Ayushman Malla — a hardware engineer and builder who enjoys thinking at the intersection of computing, design, and intelligence. I’m currently exploring how abstraction, verification, and synthesis shape the boundary between algorithms and silicon. My work revolves around FPGA design, high-level synthesis (HLS), and digital verification, with a deep curiosity about how hardware can be reimagined for AI and high-performance systems.

Recently, I worked at Transcelestial as an FPGA/Hardware Engineering Intern, developing a 10G Ethernet packet forwarder on Xilinx Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC, following an internship at AMD debugging EPYC CPU memory subsystems. Beyond hardware, I’ve built AI/ML projects ranging from financial analytics tools to deep learning models for medical imaging and autonomous vehicles — enjoying the full stack from transistor logic to inference runtime.

Outside of engineering, I find satisfaction in solving math puzzles (for some reason I am obsessed with Rubik’s cubes), watching people cook, and playing tennis. I value precision, iteration, and clarity of thought — qualities that guide how I approach both design problems and life in general.

I’m also fascinated by meta-cognition: how people structure knowledge, synthesize abstractions, and turn insight into execution. This portfolio is a space where I share my projects, reflections, and ideas — from technical breakdowns to thought experiments. At its core, it’s a record of one ongoing pursuit: to build systems, and ways of thinking, that scale.
